    Blocking

    Automatic mode starts after the page loads. Manual mode waits for Run now in the popup, then follows new content on that page until you navigate or reveal it. Saving settings resets Manual mode to waiting. Both modes require your saved rules.

    %

    90% is the default. Higher values hide fewer regions. Uncertain or failed decisions leave content visible.

    Red marks matching content, green marks retained content, and yellow marks uncertain decisions.

    Decision cache

    Turn this off to evaluate content again. You can also disable caching for individual sites in the extension popup. Changes to your rules require new decisions.

    Clearing takes effect immediately.

    Excluded sites

    Your saved rules apply according to your activation mode while enabled. Selected candidate snippets and hostnames are sent to your provider for classification. Raw HTML and form contents are excluded.

    Exclude any private pages you do not want classified. Local and internal hostnames are not supported.

    Use exact hostnames without URLs, paths, or wildcards. Subdomains need their own entry. Leave empty to block on all supported websites. You can also disable a site from the extension popup.
