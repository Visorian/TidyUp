# TidyUp

Hide unwanted parts of web pages using your own plain-language rules.

- Start with categories for ads, cookie consent, and subscription prompts, or write your own rules.
- Switch categories on or off and disable TidyUp for individual websites.
- Use **Show hidden** to reveal removed content temporarily. Click it again to hide it.

## Get started

1. Open TidyUp from your browser toolbar, then open **Settings**.
2. Under **Connection**, choose TypeSafe direct or OpenRouter and enter your API key.
3. Under **Rules**, add your own rules or expand **Starter categories** and add them. Select **Save settings**.

TidyUp runs automatically on page load. For manual control, change **When to tidy** under **Preferences**, save, then use **Run now** in the popup.

Your provider receives page snippets to check against your rules. Hiding cookie consent does not accept cookies, and hiding subscription prompts does not unlock paid content.
